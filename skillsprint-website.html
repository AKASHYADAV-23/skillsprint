The problem

4.5 crore students study in Indian colleges. Most plan in chats and earn nothing from their free hours.

Deadlines live in WhatsApp groups. Doubts wait for the next lecture. Courses teach skills but never connect to paid work. SkillSprint puts all three in one app.

Student count: AISHE 2023-24, Ministry of Education.

Plan

Add your classes and tasks. SkillSprint fits study blocks around them and re-plans the moment you add something new. Choose mornings or evenings and the day rearranges.

Exam countdowns shape your revision.

Study

Ask a doubt and get a step-by-step answer with a worked example. Still stuck? Book a senior from your campus. A 25-minute focus timer keeps the session honest.

Built around college courses, not school exams.

Earn

Short skill tracks, such as design, lead to a badge. The badge unlocks small paid gigs from local businesses. Payouts come to you through UPI.

You keep 85% of every gig.

      How it works

      From a free hour to your first payout in four steps.

      Step 1

      Add your day

      Enter your timetable, deadlines and exam dates.

      Step 2

      Follow the plan

      Study blocks appear in your free hours. Ask doubts and run focus sessions.

      Step 3

      Learn a skill

      Finish a track of short lessons and pass the badge test.

      Step 4

      Take a gig

      Apply, deliver the work and get paid.

      Skills that earn

      Learn it Tuesday. Get paid by the weekend.
